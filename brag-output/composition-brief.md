## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic` / `polished`
- Creative direction: High-octane technical launch film with cinematic zooms, tactile UI clicks, suspense sound design, and mathematical swagger.
- Interpretation: Fast-paced yet completely legible, bold monumental typography, tactile button clicks, dramatic camera zooms into real UI elements, and strict adherence to `DESIGN.md`.
- Angle: Reverse alpha blending replaces destructive generative hallucination with deterministic mathematics.
- Hook: Macro zoom into the visible watermark on an AI masterpiece with suspense sound design.
- Outro / punchline: "Zero Cloud. Zero Blurring. 100% Private."
- Avoid: Generic SaaS animations, fake 3D gradients, cartoon sounds, off-brand dark mode or floating shadows.
