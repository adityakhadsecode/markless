# Brag Plan: Markless

## What is this app?
Markless is a 100% in-browser, client-side web application that removes Google Gemini AI visible watermarks from images and Google Veo watermarks from videos using mathematically exact reverse alpha blending, zero cloud uploads, zero generative hallucinations, and lossless audio passthrough.

## The angle
Unlike destructive generative AI tools that smear, blur, or hallucinate fake pixels over watermarks, Markless uses deterministic mathematics to solve the compositor's linear alpha blending equation in reverse. It proves that clean media restoration doesn't need cloud GPUs, subscriptions, or AI hallucinations—just pure mathematics running locally inside the user's browser.

## Hook (Scene 1)
Typewriter effect types out character-by-character with mechanical keyboard sound:
"Gemini watermarked your image?"
Followed by a punchy, bouncy Mint Sprout card slam:
"NO PROBLEM!" with a celebratory sound effect.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Accurate Studio Selection**: In Scene 2, simulated mouse cursor glides directly to and clicks down on the "Image Studio" button, triggering active tactile feedback.
- **Watermark Selector Focus**: In Scene 3, the real Gemini 3.5 4K image (`galaxy_watermark.png`) loads into the workspace. The 3-Stage NCC detector locks on and the camera zooms in, focusing the 8-point selector right onto the watermark.
- **Violent Glass Shatter & 100% Clean Reveal**: In Scene 4, the watermark fractures and shatters apart into dozens of crystalline shards flying outward with a glass shatter impact sound, revealing the pristine, unblemished galaxy background from `galaxy_clean.png`.

## Outro / punchline
